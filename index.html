Why NSCC

Starting with
curiosity

I chose NSCC practically — it was local, accessible, and affordable. But I didn't come in with a clear plan. I had been around technology for years, always pulling things apart to see how they worked, and I knew I wanted more of that. NSCC's hands-on, practical approach turned out to be exactly the right fit for how I learn.

What I didn't expect was how much I'd grow beyond the curriculum. The program gave me a foundation, but curiosity pushed me further. I started exploring ethical hacking on TryHackMe and Hack The Box, completed LinkedIn Learning courses on my own time, ran virtual machine labs at home, and stayed involved in the school community at every opportunity.

I came in not knowing exactly what I wanted to do. I'm leaving with a clear sense of what I'm capable of — and a genuine excitement for wherever that takes me in IT.
